She has questions about a $3,999 certification.
Now someone is there to answer them.

An always-on AI program advisor for the Functional Medicine Certification — she meets nurse practitioners on the landing page when they hesitate, steps in when a cart is about to be abandoned, and writes the follow-up email herself.

The Problem

A $3,999 clinical credential is a considered purchase — a career pivot, a year of evenings, household money. The buyer has real questions, and today she has nowhere to ask them at the moment she's asking them.

$3,999
the highest-value single SKU in the FHEA catalog (FMP-C, sold on fhea.com & elitenp.com)
$2.8M
left in abandoned carts in July — this one certification, one website
8%
of cart abandoners ever come back and buy
1 template
the same generic "you left something in your cart" email everyone gets

Her real questions: Is it accredited? Does it count toward my prescribing requirements? Will it teach me to charge for this work? Will my employer reimburse it? — asked at 9pm on the landing page, answered by no one.

The Vision — one agent, three touchpoints

Not three disconnected automations. One advisor with a name, a consistent voice, memory of the conversation — and she says plainly that she's an AI assistant with a human colleague one message away.

1
Landing page advisor
When behavior signals hesitation — never on page load
Lingering on the price block? Opened the FAQ twice? Back for a second visit? Trying to print the page for a boss? She opens with the exact thing that person needs — and captures the lead.
2
Cart & checkout rescue
Exit intent · payment stall · coupon hunting
She names and removes the single specific blocker in the next thirty seconds — the Affirm monthly figure, the employer invoice — or simply asks permission to follow up.
3
She writes the recovery email
After abandonment · via HubSpot
The agent that had the conversation writes the email. Subject line = her actual objection. Body = the answer. The sequence stops the moment it's resolved. No templates.

Notice what she didn't do: mention price. A buyer not yet convinced the program is serious reads a price as a reason to leave. Price becomes relevant the moment rigor is resolved — and not before.

How We'll Build It

Four phases — starting with the one that needs zero website changes.

  • Phase 1The email writerAgent-written recovery emails for FM cart abandoners via HubSpot, grounded in what each person viewed. Human-approved until the team signs off. Comparison group proves the lift.
  • Phase 2The landing-page advisorChat advisor live on the fhea.com cert page with the 8 hesitation triggers, a self-updating knowledge base, lead capture and objection tagging.
  • Phase 3Cart & checkout rescueExit-intent, payment-stall, and coupon-hunt interventions at the moment of truth.
  • Phase 4Close the loop & roll outEmails continue the actual on-page conversations. Then elitenp.com, then the other five certifications.

What it is — and isn't

It is

  • One advisor across page, cart & email — with memory
  • Triggered by evidence of hesitation
  • Emails written per-person from her actual conversation
  • An honest, disclosed AI assistant
  • Starting with one product on one site

It isn't

  • A pop-up that greets everyone on page load
  • A generic abandoned-cart template
  • Something pretending to be human
  • A chatbot that makes things up
  • A big-bang launch across everything

What the three-touchpoint vision adds on top

Saves before the abandonment — the page advisor and cart rescue convert people who today never even become "abandoned carts" (invisible in the 8% math). A lead engine — hesitating visitors become captured, tagged leads ("Clinically Curious — objection: rigor") entering nurture paths that answer their actual objection. Plus a growing map of exactly what stops buyers, sharpening every page, email, and ad we run.

What it costs

Build time, AI usage (a few hundred dollars a month), and HubSpot we already own. No new hires — versus $70K+ per year, per inside sales rep, for the traditional alternative.

How we'll know it worked: advisor-assisted buyers vs. the comparison group, read out ~30 days after each phase goes live. If conversion moves, scale to the remaining five certifications and elitenp.com. If not, we've learned precisely which objections a future sales team must answer. Either way — we stop guessing.
