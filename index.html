Buyers add a certification to their cart, think "let me sleep on it"… and never come back. The normal fix is hiring an inside sales team. We'd rather not add headcount to find out if follow-up works — so we're building a salesperson out of software.

The Solution

A tireless, knowledgeable AI salesperson that reaches out personally, answers real questions with verified facts, and closes the sale.

  1. Reaches out within 15 minutesof an abandoned cart — friendly, not pushy: "Noticed you were looking at the Functional Medicine certification. Any questions I can answer?"
  2. Tells real storieshow someone just like them took the course, and what changed for their practice and income afterward.
  3. Answers their questions accurately"Will this help my practice? Is it worth $4,000? Do I have time for 80 hours?" — using only verified course facts. It never makes things up; if it doesn't know, a real person steps in.
  4. Offers 10% off at the right momentas a nudge after the conversation — including a text message — not as the opening line.
  5. Knows when to stopafter a respectful goodbye email it moves them into a gentle education track. No pestering, instant unsubscribe.
  6. Tells us what buyers are worried aboutevery question is logged — a free, always-on survey that makes our website, emails and ads smarter.

What it is — and isn't

It is

  • A follow-up salesperson made of software
  • Personal 1-on-1 emails and texts
  • Focused on closing the sale directly
  • Grounded in verified course facts only
  • Starting small: one cert (Functional Medicine), one site (FHEA)

It isn't

  • A website chat widget (Ruby already does that)
  • Mass marketing blasts (we already do those)
  • A meeting-booking tool (Phase 2, only if needed)
  • Something that invents answers
  • A big-bang launch across everything at once

How We'll Do It

A two-week build sprint starting Tuesday, Aug 4 — with safety rails the whole way.

Week 1 — Get the conversation started

Build the "brain": a knowledge base of everything about the certification — what's included, who it's for, honest answers to the five real objections (why does this matter · will it help me grow · what makes it different · do I have the time · is it worth the cost). It updates itself when course pages change — nobody feeds it manually. Then build the message sequence — 6 emails + 1 text — reviewed and approved by our team before anything goes out, and wire it to HubSpot so it fires automatically on cart abandonment.

Week 2 — Make it talk back

When someone replies, the AI answers accurately within minutes — or hands off to a real person instead of guessing. And we build the scoreboard: a live dashboard of conversions, revenue recovered, and what buyers are asking.

Safety rails: a human approves every template before launch and the first ~50 AI replies · texts only go to people who opted in · unsubscribes honored instantly · a comparison group stays on the old process so we can prove the improvement is real, not luck.

Where it scales from here

What it costs

~2 weeks of build time, AI usage fees (a few hundred dollars a month), and HubSpot texting costs. The alternative — an inside sales team — starts at $70K+ per rep per year before they close a single sale.

How we'll know it worked: within ~30 days of launch we compare the AI-assisted group against the comparison group. If conversion moves, we scale. If it doesn't, we've spent two weeks and learned exactly what stops buyers — either way, we stop guessing.
